# GDPR Compliance Summary (Mindful Cradle)

This document summarizes the GDPR-related features implemented in the app. The goal is to give users clear control over their data and to meet GDPR requirements in a simple, user-friendly way.

## Work Completed

1. Clear consent before account creation

- Users are shown a consent screen explaining what data is collected and why.
- Users must actively agree before they can create an account.
- The consent screen is available in both English and Sinhala.

2. Right to delete account

- Users can delete their account from the app.
- Deleting the account removes their personal data and questionnaire history.
- A confirmation step is required to avoid accidental deletion.

3. Right to export data

- Users can download a copy of their data.
- The export is saved as a file on their device for easy access.
- A preview is shown so users can understand what will be downloaded.

4. Privacy and transparency

- The app explains what information is collected and how it is used.
- The data and privacy section is accessible from the profile area.
- All messages are presented in simple language for end users.

## Outcome

These changes add clear consent, easy data export, and account deletion inside the app. The user journey remains simple while meeting GDPR expectations.
